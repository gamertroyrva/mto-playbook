Math Trade Organizer Playbook

Draft table format — Phase 1: Awareness & Recruiting. Round 1.

Phase 1 — Awareness & Recruiting
Task Task Description Task Steps Why Do This
Venue Lock-In Securing the in-person swap venue.
  1. Confirm date, time, and location for the in-person swap event with the venue (e.g., Unplugged Board Games Cafe).

Every other Phase 1 activity references swap-day details. Locking the venue first means the schedule, the Facebook post, and outreach can all speak with confidence instead of "TBD."
Build the Trade Schedule Establish the full trade schedule and document it in the math trade project plan document.
  1. Set dates for the GeekList window (open/close), the wants/submission window, the results announcement, and the swap event.
  2. Record the full schedule in the math trade project plan document.

Troy prefers a two-week Offer Up Games phase to allow newbies to be found and still have time to join.

This schedule becomes the backbone of the Facebook Announcement Post and everything that follows.
Facebook Announcement Post A post to the Richmond, VA Board Games Facebook group, 2-3 weeks ahead of the GeekList launch (refer to prior FB MT announcements to avoid recreating the wheel).
  1. Draft a post with the full trade timeline, framed for returners and newcomers alike (emphasize flexibility — any number of games).
  2. Post to the Richmond, VA Board Games Facebook group.
  3. Point questions to Discord (comment with invite link).
  4. Promise a follow-up post when the GeekList opens.

This is where the RVA board gamer community lives — the highest-leverage single post of the phase. It plants the flag and seeds the Phase 2 follow-up post, so the two phases work together rather than duplicating effort.
Sing from the Mountain Tops An open-ended, improvisational task — whatever it takes to extend the trade's reach beyond the core RVA community.
  1. Reach out directly to contacts in other regions (e.g., via Discord DM) and ask them to spread the word in their own communities.
  2. Review past event attendee lists (e.g., RiVirCon manifest) for people outside the RVA metro with an existing relationship, and reach out directly (e.g., email).
  3. Loop in participants from other recent trades (e.g., Eastern USA math trade).
  4. Devise and act on additional outreach ideas as they come to mind.

This extends the trade's reach beyond what's strictly necessary to run one, which means more math trade magic for everyone involved.

Scope note: this reflects the full "Troy edition" of running this trade. A minimal viable Phase 1 is the first two tasks — Venue Lock-In and Build the Trade Schedule — plus the Facebook Announcement Post. Sing from the Mountain Tops is a discretionary layer: regional relay contacts, mining past event attendee lists, and looping in other trades' participants are all extra effort beyond what's required, layered on by an organizer who wants the trade to shine for everyone involved.
